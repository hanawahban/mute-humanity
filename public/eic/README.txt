Put each record's EIC clip here as <record>/clip.mp4 (ocean, plants, rain, no2, fires, plastic).
Optional: <record>/colorbar.json = {"stops": [[r, g, b, value0to1], ...]} to decode colours into real values.
Clips must be served by this app (not hot-linked from svs.gsfc.nasa.gov), or the browser blocks reading their pixels.
